# Control Centre Access

Access is named-user only. Do not share accounts. Platform Owners approve roles; Platform Administrators cannot grant or remove owner access. Support, Security and Read-only roles remain constrained to their documented areas.

Review active administrators and sessions at least quarterly and after staff/contractor changes. Revoke all sessions after suspected compromise. Sensitive changes require a case or incident reason. Administrative notes are operational records, not a substitute for audit events.

Break-glass use requires two people, a recorded incident, the narrowest duration possible, session revocation and credential rotation afterward. DNS secrecy is never an access control.
