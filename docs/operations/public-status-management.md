# Public Status Management

Operators create an incident with a specific affected public service, customer-safe title/message, state, explicit confirmation and internal audit reason. Updates and resolution are permanently audited. Do not paste stack traces, provider identifiers, ticket secrets, internal hosts or security investigation detail into customer messages.

Use Operational, Degraded Performance, Partial Outage, Major Outage or Maintenance according to customer impact. Update during material changes and resolve only after customer-facing recovery is confirmed. The public page retains a 90-day recent-history view; database retention follows the retention standard.
