# Future Wish Lists

Wish Lists are a first-class coordination feature for birthdays, Christmas, anniversaries and other occasions.

## Intended capabilities

- Personal and occasion-based lists
- Internal sharing within a Home
- Optional revocable external share links
- Gift reservation without revealing the reserver to the recipient
- Priority levels
- Product or idea links
- Reservation and purchase states
- Reminders tied to occasions

## Boundaries

MyKhaya does not sell products, operate a marketplace or host product imagery. External links are references only.

## Security requirements

External shares must use high-entropy revocable tokens stored as hashes, optional expiry, strict rate limits, minimal returned data, abuse monitoring and no access to wider Home information.
