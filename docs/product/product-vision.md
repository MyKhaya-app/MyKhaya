# Product Vision

## Purpose

MyKhaya helps the people closest to each other coordinate everyday life together.

## Positioning

MyKhaya is a private consumer coordination product. It is not a corporate workspace, infrastructure tool, social network or storage service.

## Product test

Every proposed feature must answer:

> **Does this make life together simpler?**

A related, more concrete check lives in `docs/engineering/engineering-standards.md`: every new module must justify its existence on the Home screen. If it has nothing glanceable to show there, that's a signal to reconsider it, not a UI gap to patch over.

## Core use cases

- Shared calendars and occasions
- Tasks and chores
- Shopping lists
- Meal planning
- Plans and availability
- Birthdays and important dates
- Wish Lists and gift reservation
- Household and close-friend coordination
- Reminders, notifications, notes and polls

## Explicit exclusions

MyKhaya must not become a cloud drive, photo library, receipt archive, insurance manager, password manager, medical-record platform, financial platform, public social network or business project-management tool.

## Experience principles

MyKhaya should feel warm, calm, private, friendly, modern, trustworthy and uncluttered. People open it to organise life, not administer a system.
