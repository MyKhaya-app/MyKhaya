# Incident Response Outline

1. Detect and record the incident.
2. Preserve relevant evidence and logs.
3. Contain affected credentials, sessions, services or releases.
4. Triage severity and assess impacted Homes, users, children’s information, processors and data.
5. Preserve evidence with chain-of-custody notes; do not destroy relevant audit/security records.
6. Eradicate the cause and patch safely.
7. Restore from tested sources where needed.
8. Complete a documented personal-data risk assessment. Record the notification decision, UK GDPR timing, ICO reporting considerations and reasons.
9. Notify affected users clearly where legally required or operationally appropriate, without compromising containment.
10. Complete remediation, regression testing and post-incident review.
11. Update standards, controls, status communications and monitoring.
