# Initial DPIA — Human Review Required

This structured assessment is not legal advice or a completed DPIA. The controller must identify lawful bases, consult the DPO/adviser where applicable, assess necessity/proportionality, record residual risk acceptance and determine whether ICO consultation is required.

## Processing in scope

Hosted family coordination; account/Home relationships; parent-managed child profiles; invitations; email and future notifications; administrative metadata access; authentication, security and operational logging; future external sharing.

## Data and people

Adults, invited people and children may be represented. Data includes identity/contact details, household relationships, coordination content, device/session metadata and security/administrative records. Relationship and children’s data can reveal sensitive circumstances even when not special-category data.

## Necessity and safeguards

Use high-privacy defaults, minimised fields, Home isolation, explicit membership, no location tracking, no independent child login, no child external communication, no operator impersonation, no casual content access, encryption in transit, hashed secrets, constrained roles, audited admin actions and retention limits.

## Misuse and high-impact scenarios

Risks include coercive household access, unsafe invitations, exposure after relationship changes, child-data overcollection, credential takeover, insider browsing, bulk metadata export, notification disclosure, misleading status, backup loss and cross-Home authorization failure. Mitigations require product abuse review, rapid membership/session revocation, independent testing, managed operator access, monitoring, deletion/export workflows and processor due diligence.

## Open decisions

Complete data mapping and lawful-basis analysis; age/parental-responsibility design; processor and international-transfer assessment; retention approval; abuse/support procedures; WebAuthn; independent penetration/privacy testing; data-subject workflows; and sign-off by accountable humans.
